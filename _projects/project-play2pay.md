A global payment platform that allows mobile phone users to collect points by playing games, watching videos and completing special offers on their devices; these points can be later redeemed for service provider bill credits.\
[Play2Pay](https://play2pay.com/)

A global payment platform that lets mobile users earn points by playing games, watching videos, and completing special offers on their devices. These points can be redeemed for service-provider bill credits (especially valuable in prepaid models) or gift cards (for post-paid carriers such as T-Mobile).
Key capabilities

Earn rewards through games, video content, offers, surveys, and other engagement activities
Redeem points for direct bill credits or gift cards
Available on both Android and iOS

## Tech stack

- Android: Android Studio, Java (earlier versions), Kotlin, Room
- iOS: Flutter, Dart, Xcode, Swift

## Markets

- Initially launched in the US (Cricket), Mexico (Unefon), UK (Lyca), Indonesia (Indosat), and Brazil (Tim)
- Currently available in the US for Cricket, T-Mobile, and Metro by T-Mobile users

## Technical challenges & solutions

Built the iOS version from scratch using Flutter to deliver a native-feeling cross-platform experience, delivering a fully functional MVP within a tight two-month timeline.
